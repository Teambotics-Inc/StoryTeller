# PMI (Philip Morris International): research log

All sources accessed 2026-10-02. Fetched as raw text with `scripts/fetch-text.mjs`, except the key-milestones timeline (year labels read from the rendered page's `data-title` attributes in a browser).

## Assumptions made without asking (stated in the final message)

- **Subject**: Philip Morris International (PMI), the company, as it describes and is described today. Not Altria / Philip Morris USA, except where they intersect.
- **Kind**: company. **Affiliation**: independent observer; the user is not stated to be affiliated. Credit carries no personal name.
- **Angle**: PMI says it is building a smoke-free future and will one day replace cigarettes. The map sets that stated vision beside what regulators, health bodies and its own annual reports say, so a reader can see both the transformation and the contested parts.
- **Boundaries**: 1847 to mid-2026. Litigation history, tobacco farming/labour detail, and per-country market data are out of scope.
- **Brand**: PMI's own site is white with blue; a light blue theme is derived from it (see theme.json). No logo embedded.

## Source tiers

1. Primary: pmi.com pages (company claims), FDA and WHO documents (regulators / health body).
2. Secondary: Tobacco Tactics (University of Bath, Tobacco Control Research Group), an academic tobacco-industry watchdog with an explicit critical stance. Treated as a tier 2 source with a point of view; its claims are attributed.
3. Tertiary: Wikipedia (orientation, and the disputed Marlboro launch date).

### ms: "Key milestones" (pmi.com), tier 1 (company)
- https://www.pmi.com/who-we-are/key-milestones
- 1847: "Mr. Philip Morris opens a shop on London's Bond Street, selling tobacco and ready-made cigarettes."
- 1908: "Marlboro, which went on to become the company's most famous brand, is established and registered as a trademark."
- 1972: "Marlboro becomes the world's best-selling cigarette."
- 1987: "PMI is incorporated as an operating company of Philip Morris Companies Inc."
- 2000: "PMI calls for regulation of the tobacco industry at the World Health Organization's public hearings on the Framework Convention for Tobacco Control in Geneva, Switzerland."
- 2001: operations centre moved from Rye Brook, NY to Lausanne, Switzerland.
- 2005: acquires PT HM Sampoerna (Indonesia) and Coltabaco (Colombia); China National Tobacco Company licensed production of Marlboro.
- 2009: new R&D facility in Neuchatel, Switzerland, "over 400 scientists, specialists, and staff", for smoke-free products.
- 2013: strategic framework with Altria on e-cigarettes / candidate smoke-free products.
- 2014: "PMI pilots IQOS, its first heat-not-burn platform in its smoke-free products portfolio, in Italy and Japan."
- 2015: IQOS launched in key cities in Portugal, Romania, Russia, Switzerland.
- 2016: MRTP applications for IQOS submitted to FDA; "PMI announces its vision to deliver a smoke-free future".
- 2017: PMTA applications for IQOS submitted to FDA.
- 2019: "The FDA authorizes IQOS for sale in the U.S." (PMTA filed 2017).
- 2020: FDA authorizes IQOS 2.4 as a modified risk tobacco product with reduced exposure information; Board reaffirms purpose of delivering a smoke-free future.
- 2021: acquires Fertin Pharma, OtiTopic and Vectura ("growth opportunities beyond products that provide nicotine").
- 2022: BONDS by IQOS; agreement with Altria to end their IQOS commercial relationship in the U.S. (full rights from April 2024); acquires Swedish Match; HQ moved from New York City to Stamford, Connecticut.
- 2023: "PMI announces ambition for over two-thirds of its total net revenues to come from better alternatives to smoking by 2030."
- 2025: "FDA authorizes all ZYN nicotine pouch products currently marketed by Swedish Match in the U.S."
- 2026: "The U.S. FDA issues modified risk tobacco product orders for 20 ZYN nicotine pouch products".
- Page footer: "Our progress toward a smoke-free future as of H1 2026": 42 percent of total net revenues from smoke-free; "estimated legal-age consumers of PMI's smoke-free products" over 43 million (as of Dec 31, 2025). Company claims.

### tt: "Philip Morris International", Tobacco Tactics (Univ. of Bath, TCRG), tier 2 (critical watchdog)
- https://www.tobaccotactics.org/article/philip-morris-international/ (updated 2026-10-01)
- "Despite its 'smoke-free' strategy, the majority of PMI's revenue – around 59% in 2025 – comes from the sale of cigarettes and other conventional combustible tobacco products."
- 2024 annual report: cigarette shipments "rose to 616.8 billion sticks, up from 612.9 billion in 2023." In 2023 PMI "abandoned its own target to ship fewer than 550 billion units of combustible products by 2025".
- TCRG 2022 analysis of a leaked strategy document: "In public-facing communications, PMI stress their commitment to transformation and change, while in investor-facing communications, they focus on cigarettes and reiterate the strength of their existing cigarette brand portfolios."
- Altria / IQOS: PMI paid Altria US$2.7 billion; IQOS rights transferred to PMI on 30 April 2024; went on sale in Austin, Texas in March 2025.
- Swedish Match: May 2022 offer, US$16 billion; PMI CEO said "the opportunity in the U.S., which is the world's largest market for smoke-free products."
- Vectura sold in September 2024 "after strong criticism of the acquisition from the WHO and others", PMI citing "unwarranted opposition to PMI's transformation".
- Policy opposition (as reported by Tobacco Tactics): 2014 legal challenges to EU Tobacco Products Directive; 2017 challenge to plain packaging in Uruguay; leaked 2013 documents on lobbying against the EU TPD and UK plain packaging.
- Global share: around 23% of the global cigarette market outside China (Euromonitor, 2025).
- Foundation for a Smoke-Free World: launched Sept 2017; PMI ceased funding in 2023; renamed Global Action to End Smoking in May 2024.
