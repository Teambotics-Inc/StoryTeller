# Research log — From Chatbots to Operators

## Scope and editorial angle

Research period: 2024-11-25 through 2026-11-04, with the main narrative window in 2025–2026. The story tracks a capability transition: generated answers → reasoning → multimodal perception → tool use → software operation → shared, longer-running work. “Operator” is used as a shorthand for bounded systems that can plan and act through software with authorization and review; it is not a claim of general autonomy.

## Discovery trail

TLDR newsletters in the author’s connected mailbox were used as a discovery index for candidate announcements, including Operator, GPT-5, Claude for Chrome, Gemini Computer Use, Gemini 3, GPT-5.2-Codex, Codex’s broader computer/app workflows, workspace agents, and DevDay 2026. The mailbox was not used as evidence: private message identifiers, addresses, and raw newsletter text are intentionally absent from this repository. Each material claim was checked against the public primary source recorded in `story.json`.

## Uncertainties and guardrails

- Vendor benchmark numbers and product claims are reproduced only as reported and are not independently audited here.
- Release announcements establish what a vendor announced, not universal availability, reliability, or production readiness.
- “Autonomy” is deliberately bounded in the graph: the evidence supports increasingly capable workflows with permissions, sandboxes, confirmations, evaluations, and human takeover—not a settled claim of general autonomy.
- Some edges are editorial bridges between independently documented milestones. They are marked `inferred` so the reader can distinguish synthesis from a relationship directly stated by a source.
